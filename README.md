# 🇳🇵 Nepal Political Fight — Ultra 3D Arcade Edition

> Created & Modded by **[PZ48HCE](https://github.com/PZ48HCE)** | 🎮 **[Play Live](https://pz48hce.github.io/nepal-political-fight/)**
>
> An upgraded 3D satirical arcade fighting game featuring **authentic rigged 3D character models**, **real character voices + viral video/reel meme sound effects**, and **modern PC controls (`WASD` + `Space` + `Shift` + Mouse)**.

---

## ⚡ Features & Upgrades

1. **Authentic Rigged 3D Characters**:
   - High-poly skeletal-rigged `.glb` models with `EXT_meshopt_compression` and inverse kinematics:
     - 🏗️ **Balen Shah** (`models/new-balen-rigged.glb`): *Dozer Shakti* & floating currency particles
     - 🚢 **KP Sharma Oli** (`models/new-kp-rigged.glb`): *Jahaj (Ship) & Rail Shakti*
     - 🪨 **Harka Sampang** (`models/new-harka-rigged.glb`): *Dharan Stone & Water Shakti*
     - 🎙️ **Ashika Tamang** (`models/new-ashika-rigged.glb`): *Live Stream & Mic Shakti*
   - Dual-slot animated 3D preview stages during fighter selection with dynamic lighting and shadows.

2. **Real Character Voices + Viral Video & Reel Sound Effects**:
   - **Real Fighter Voices**:
     - Every fighter shouts and reacts with their own real voice lines for **punches**, **kicks**, **blocks**, **hurt recoils**, and **6-second Super speeches**.
   - **Viral Meme & Reel SFX**:
     - 👋 **Comedic Slap** (`meme-slap`) & 💥 **Anime Punch** (`anime-punch`) on punch combos
     - 🔨 **BONK!** (`bonk`) on heavy kicks, sweeps & roundhouse strikes
     - 😒 **"BRUH"** (`bruh`) & 🔔 **Metal Pipe Clang** (`metal-pipe`) when blocking or parrying attacks
     - 💣 **Vine Boom** (`vine-boom`) on Super impacts & devastating knockdowns
     - 😂 **Viral Nepali Meme Laugh** (`nepal-laugh`) & **"Aye Haye Haye"** (`aye-haye`) on KO & round victories!

3. **Modern PC Gamer Control Panel (`WASD` + `Space` + Mouse)**:
   - Visual keyboard & mouse diagram modal accessible via **`🎮 CONTROLS`** in the top menu.
   - Dedicated jump via **`Spacebar`** (or `W`), dash/evade via **`Shift`** (or `Q`).
   - Full mouse combat: **Left Mouse Click (LMB)** for Punch, **Right Mouse Click (RMB)** for Kick, **Middle Mouse Click** for Guard.
   - Persistent reactive bottom HUD bar (`#control-bar`) that highlights pressed keys in real time.

## 🎮 Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Alternate / Mouse |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Move Left / Right** | <kbd>A</kbd> / <kbd>D</kbd> | <kbd>←</kbd> / <kbd>→</kbd> |
| **Jump** | <kbd>SPACEBAR</kbd> | <kbd>W</kbd> / <kbd>↑</kbd> |
| **Crouch / Duck** | <kbd>S</kbd> | <kbd>↓</kbd> |
| **Dash / Evade** | <kbd>SHIFT</kbd> | <kbd>Q</kbd> |
| **Punch** | <kbd>J</kbd> | **Left Click (LMB)** / <kbd>F</kbd> |
| **Kick** | <kbd>K</kbd> | **Right Click (RMB)** / <kbd>G</kbd> |
| **Guard / Parry** | <kbd>E</kbd> | **Middle Click (MMB)** / <kbd>I</kbd> |
| **Super Move** | <kbd>L</kbd> | <kbd>R</kbd> (100% Super Meter) |
